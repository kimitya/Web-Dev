Recipe #1

  • Time: 14
  • Serves: 6
Ingredients Quantity
Cream Milk 1 full cup
Nutella 1/3 cup

Step 1: Gather all ingredients and popsicle moulds.

Step 2: Place the milk and Nutella in a blender and blend until thoroughly combined.

Step 3: Pour into a popsicle moulds.

Step 4: Freeze and serve.

Recipe #2

  • Time: 3
  • Serves: 3
Ingredients Quantity
Bananas (frozen,peeled,sliced) 4
Dark brown sugar 1/3 cup
Low-fat Greek yoghurt 250g

Step 1: Place all ingredients into a food processor and process until smooth. Serve immediately.

Recipe #3

  • Time: 14
  • Serves: 20
Ingredients Quantity
White chocolate melts 1 cup
pretzels 20

Step 1:Line a tray with baking paper. Melt the chocolate in a double boiler over a saucepan of simmering water or in the microwave.

Step 2:Place in a deep bowl and then just dip each pretzel in the chocolate so it comes up halfway. Set aside to cool on the tray and serve.
